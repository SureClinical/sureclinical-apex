Locations

Reno, Nevada & Lake Tahoe

Tired of the traffic, insane housing prices, and stress of Silicon Valley? Consider a move to SureClinical’s headquarters, located in the scenic Reno/Lake Tahoe area.
Make SureClinical your hub for adventure and discovery! Learn more below about the activities and resources available in the the Reno Tahoe area.

The great outdoors is central to what makes Reno so special – find adventure everywhere:  From pristine Lake Tahoe to the Truckee River and beyond, Reno Tahoe has something for everyone.

Let it Snow

Reno sees an average snowfall of 22 inches per year and has a base elevation of 4,500 ft. Want more powder? Lake Tahoe boasts an average of 18 feet per year at 6,200 ft. elevation!

Things to Do

Reno is famous for its small town feel and big city fun. Wander through Reno's active downtown, midtown, and Riverwalk Districts, stopping to admire the city's public art and murals along the way. Reno has all the amenities of a city twice its size, without the traffic and inflated prices.
