Our Story

Founded by experts in clinical operations and enterprise software, we knew all too well the pain points of manual processes, the stacks of documents, the rows of file cabinets, and the never ending mail and email deliveries, coupled with disconnected legacy applications.  We knew there had to be a better way to work faster, easier and smarter, with better results. 

Before we started, we listened to prospective users and we asked a lot of questions.  We learned that  our clinical platform and applications should: 

  • Automate  processes to eliminate manual tasks like wet signing, scanning, uploading, classifying and storing clinical documents and forms.
  • Connect disconnected clinical teams
  • Connect disconnected applications
  • Accelerate team collaboration with centralized clinical intelligence and data
  • Be scalable to support thousands of users anywhere
  • Be flexible to allow changes without programming
  • Be based on open standards for clinical data sharing with any system anywhere
  • Be easy to use and easy to learn
  • Be available everywhere - mobile, web or desktop

Leveraging our experience in clinical trial operations and enterprise systems, we set out to develop SureClinical.  After two years of design, development, usability testing and validation, we introduced our open platform and SureETMF, a regulatory content management solution to automate, streamline and accelerate clinical trial operations. 

Word of mouth marketing and some early CRO partnerships helped us to gain momentum.  Fast forwarding six years later to 2021, Our Clinical Applications and Platform currently supports over 600 clinical trials globally, with an ever growing customer base.    

With headquarters in Reno,  North America and a global presence across the Americas, Europe and Asia-Pacific, we serve customers globally in biotechnology, pharmaceuticals, medical devices and clinical research.

 With a heritage built on listening to customers, innovating and delivering amazing software products and solutions, we are continuously evolving and enhancing our offerings to be the best value and user experience available. 

Whether you’re a sponsor, a CRO, a medical research organization or site investigator – we empower you to work faster, work easier and work smarter, anytime, anywhere.
